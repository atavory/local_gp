\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Distributed and local optimization.}
Federated averaging and local SGD reduce communication by taking multiple
local steps before synchronization \citep{mcmahan2017communication,
stich2018local,li2020federated}. Subsequent work improves robustness and
stability under heterogeneity or long synchronization intervals
\citep{karimireddy2020scaffold,reddi2021adaptive,douillard2023diloco,
naganuma2025pseudo}. \method is complementary: it changes what the server does
with parallel candidate updates, using the round as an HPO opportunity rather
than only as a variance-reduction or communication-saving device.

\paragraph{Hyperparameter optimization.}
Classical HPO methods allocate compute across independent configurations using
random search, Bayesian optimization, or multi-fidelity bandit schedules
\citep{bergstra2012random,snoek2012practical,li2017hyperband,
falkner2018bohb}. Online HPO and optimizer selection study nonstationary or
adaptive choices during training \citep{kang2023onlinehpo,
naganuma2023optimizer}. Our setting differs in the aggregation target: the
arms are scored inside a distributed round and their evidence updates one
shared trajectory.

\paragraph{Weight averaging and model merging.}
Averaging model weights or checkpoints can improve generalization and combine
solutions \citep{izmailov2018averaging,wortsman2022model,verma2024merging}.
Population and parameter-averaging methods also use multiple trajectories or
models as a source of diversity \citep{jolicoeur2023papa}. \method focuses on
the online optimizer step rather than post-hoc merging: candidate states are
created from the current round and immediately selected or aggregated.

\paragraph{Validation probes and controls.}
Validation losses are routinely used for early stopping, model selection, and
HPO. The question here is whether validation-like probes are cheap enough and
localized enough to guide same-round distributed aggregation. Our shuffled
controls and exchangeability null are meant to distinguish genuine
hyperparameter label content from probe noise and stochastic filtering.
